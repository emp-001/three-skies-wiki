***Itolkanwa*, or the League of Three Copper Skies**, usually known as just Three Skies, is a federation of a dozen nations spanning over half of the continent. It is the largest and most powerful empire the world has seen, rivalled only by [[Uwced]] in the distant north.

## Origins

Three Skies was formed by the nations of [[Tuklua]], [[Iruchu]] and [[Prakan]] in the west of the continent after they defeated the conquests of La-Kha in OR 184. Originally a defensive and trade pact built around the three nations shared religion in the True Faith, the past two centuries have seen the federation expand into a continent-spanning empire with its own military, governors, and trading outposts.

The League of Three Copper Skies gets its name from the battle where La-Kha was defeated. The Battle of the Fork was fought where the Krina and Ukul rivers meet, at the frontier of all three nations, and it is said the dusk sky at the climax of the battle was a particularly rich orange.

## Geography

Three Skies extends over a vast territory, stretching from Iruchu in the west to [[Aroen]] in the north, containing rocky highlands, mangrove forests, river valleys, dry chaparrals, and many other biomes.

## Leadership

Officially, Three Skies still relies on its core member nations to exist. 

In practice, it is a political power all its own, directly controlling its capital Anwakri, one of the largest and wealthiest cities on the continent, its surrounding lands called the Free Estates, which are rich farmland, and hundreds of river trading posts, towns and forts across the empire called *Ayma.*

Its leadership has three parts. 

First is the Council, which has seven members: two representatives each from Tuklua, Iruchu and Prakan, and a single representative from the Shusa people. Councilors are selected by the leaders of these nations respectively.

Second, is the *Rokaw*, or Parliament. A kind of lower house, it was established as a concession after the league's civil war, and represents the elites from the league's member states. It meets twice yearly, and by tradition, any recognised ruler may attend, if they can prove their wealth, blood or station. The Rokaw doesn't have any real legislative power, but is used by the Council .

Third is the *Eprat*, or Keeper. Originally an administrative position tasked by the Council with managing taxes and assignments, it now serves as the *de facto* leader of the league, with the power to set its direction, declare war, direct the River Fleet, and raise tariffs and taxes for major projects. 

This is an informal arrangement that has existed since the Tayran reforms, decades ago, and relies on a strong-willed Keeper being able to leverage the Council.

There are also the regional *Mura*, or governors, who control Three Skie's territories in Latti, Chupash, and also the Etetipawan (where they are known as Triarchs). They have significant regional power, and carry significant sway back in Anwakri. They are appointed by the Council and serve until they are dismissed.

## Power
##### The River Fleet

The main source of Three Skies' power is its control over the continent's vast river network, which it maintains with its famous River Fleet.

The River Fleet has existed since the early days of the league, and for many is the most tangible part of its presence. Consisting largely of longer, shallow-draft warships, dozens of major fleets and countless small units are stationed all over the empire, using a network of waterways and canals to respond to any threat within hours or days.

What makes the Tolkan River Fleet unique is its scale and logistics. Shipyards in Ankwakri and Prakan are capable of producing warships on an assembly line, at a rate of one ship per day. 

Meanwhile, the *riparines*, or river-warriors trained by the Tuklut on Lake Koba are among the most feared warriors on the continent. Nothing can match the fleet's resilience and ferocity.

##### Ayma

To project its reach across the continent, Three Skies relies on fortified outposts placed along rivers, called *Ayma*. These range in size from single buildings used as service stations, to full cities, and are always garrisoned by River Fleet riparines, rather than local soldiers.

Ayma are multi-purpose, being used as places of shelter for the local population, service stations for couriers and traders, watchtowers, garrisons, markets, and so on.
