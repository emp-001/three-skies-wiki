##### ***Marshland Farmers***

***Pempi Chupawa*, or the Territory of Chupash** is one of [[Three Skies]]' vassals. Located in a marshy region along the Salted Coast, it is an unlikely breadbasket, with its saltwater crops and spirulina being shipped all across the empire. An open-minded people, the Chupash live in relative harmony with the Ukmaw, a tribe of sea peoples who settled in their lands a century ago.

## Origins

The Chupachi believe they were originally crabs living along the briny Salted Coast, who were given their standing forms and the gift of thought after saving the storm god Kech after a battle with a sea beast, tending to her wounds by weaving bandages out of seaweed and salve from algae.

Kech was unpredictable, some years treating them as honoured companions and bestowing gifts and plenty, and other years lashing out in anger, destroying their homes and casting them aside. This pattern soon shaped the relationship between the Chupachi and the natural world, which they view as cyclical; neither good nor bad.

The Ukmaw, one of the Sea Peoples, settled in Chupash during the 200s OR, as part of an agreement with Tipjat and Three Skies.
##### Three Skies

For centuries, Chupash was a vassal state of the old Kiwan Empire, providing a yearly tribute of food. When Three Skies conquered old Kiwa, their many villages and towns agreed to pay tribute to Three Skies, in exchange for the protection of their River Fleet from raiders and bandits. Today, they are a full territory of Three Skies, which maintains a small garrison in a several *Ayma*, or river-fortresses.
 

## Geography

Chupash is found to the immediate east of [[Hachua]] and south of [[Etetipawan|the Etetipawan]], along the Salted Coast. It is very flat, and dominated by salt marshes, lakes and mangrove forests.

Chupachi settlements are generally smaller, perched on stilts or whatever steady ground can be found. The land is scattered with floating farms and paddies in and around the marshes.

The largest settlement is Semrima, build around the Three Skies Ayma on the Lake of Plenty, known for its bright green hue from its extensive spirulina farms.

The Chupachi are masterful farmers, producing huge amounts of salty vegetables well in excess of their needs. Their spirulina in particular is a key ration of the Tolkan River Fleet.

Flooding is frequent in Chupash, as are heavy monsoon rain and violent storms.

## Leadership

The Chupachi are a territory ruled directly by a *Mura*, or governor, from Three Skies. Three Skies take a light touch, extracting tribute in food and largely allowing the locals to live how they see fit, provided they do not spread the worship of false gods.

Each village in Chupach is led by a sort of ealdorman called a *Rachi*, who is elected by the village elders. They have to be over a certain age, and demonstrate their competence to lead to the whole village. Village meetings are led by the Rachi daily, where grievances may be heard, news spread, and gossip traded.

## Religion  
  
Though most Chupachi have publicly converted to the [[True Faith]], many still believe in the lessons of the storm god Kech and the eternal cycle of struggle and prosperity. Now Kech is viewed as evil or at least abusive and unworthy of worship, the people see their new god as just another part of this cycle.

A handful of open adherents to Kech remain in isolated villages.

## Culture  
 

##### Values

The Chupachi live in a land prone to constant flooding and violent storms. One year may be peaceful and prosperous, whilst the next brings chaos and death. Because of this, they recognise the value of adaptability and community; being rigid and insular is seen as a good way to die, stubborn and alone.

The Chupachi are seen as open-minded sorts, always looking for new ways to grow food or craft, and are accepting of outsiders who can demonstrate their utility, meaning the Ukmaw integrated better here than any of the other sea peoples.

##### Gender

In line with their philosophy of adaptability and the cyclical nature of fortune, it is common for Chupachi to change genders several times in their life, to suit the needs of the community at the time. If more children are needed, some may become Kimas, whilst in times of struggle and strife, the land may swell with Dams and Tous.

This is begrudgingly tolerated by their Tolkan overlords, who believe gender is conveyed by the Virtuous Aspects, only once.

##### Dress & Appearance

Chupachi have a pale red, gritty skin that looks like it is flecked with rock salt, and dark eyes. Those with Ukmaw heritage look a little different, with bluer undertones and grey eyes.

Their sense of fashion is broadly utilitarian. Most people wear only very limited, light clothing, instead coating their bodies in a dark, oily substance called *Lok* made from mangrove sap that provides a watertight and warm seal over their bodies. At a formal gathering, many might wear only this, a loincloth, cloak and sandals, and even less if they are working.

Lok is scraped off every week in warm water to clean.

Though largely assimilated, the Ukmaw have left their mark on the fashion of the Chupachi, bringing into mode wicker hats with broad rims, used to fend off the sun.

##### Customs

Wind chimes made of brass and wood are common, said to be a symbol of the storm god Kech.

People are encouraged to travel to villages far away to find a mate, and foreign partners are even more highly prized. 'Mate-pilgrimages' to secure a partner, especially one with skills not known in the village, are a coming-of-age ritual.

Because of this, the Chupachi regularly interact with other cultures, meaning that while most will speak their native tongue Hupak, most can speak decent Sicyhur.  It is even common for descendants of foreign partners to speak their parent's tongue for generations. This is because second languages are seen as a status symbol - they show that one is not inbred and is open-minded enough to have travelled.

Foreigners and visitors are treated warmly, but they can expect to be grilled by village elders for news, knowledge and skills that they can impart to help the settlement.

## Economy

Chupachi is an efficient agrarian economy, producing a huge surplus of saltwater crops and spirulina, which is exported far and wide across the empire. Beyond this, the assimilated Ukmaw produce wickerwork that is prized for its quality.

## Relations  
  
Chupachi are well known as saltwater farmers and respected for the bounty they bring in, though some in core Three Skies view them as rubes. They have a respect for Three Skies, who they view as protectors, though tensions have risen in recent years following the tightening grip of their Tolkan occupiers over the region.
