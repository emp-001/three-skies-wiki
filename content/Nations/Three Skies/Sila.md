##### ***Master Builders***

*This article is a stub. It may be expanded on in future.*

***Ken Salewa*, or the Tributary of Sila**, is one of [[Three Skies]]' vassals. Once a kingdom on the frontier of the Kiwan and [[Uwced]] empires bent on resisting their influence, their lands are scattered with imposing stone fortifications, roads and towers, for they are the best builders on the continent. Now a tributary to Three Skies, craftsfolk from Sila can be found all over the empire, and they were vital to the construction of the Great Canal.

Sila sits on the hilly eastern edge of the of the Great Bay, nestled into the Goejon Mountains. [[Aroen]] lies to its north, and the [[Toemo|Kingdom of Toemo]] to its west, and it is watered by the Loue river. Its coastline is where the Great Bay and Blood Coast meet.

##### Three Skies

Sila submitted to Three Skies half a century ago, after its infant king was forced to pledge tribute by the invading River Fleet. Today, it is a reliable tributary with a pliant king.
