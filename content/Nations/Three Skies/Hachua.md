##### ***Upland Archers***

*This article is a stub. It may be expanded on in future.*

***Ken Hachwa*, or the Tributary of Hachua**, is one of [[Three Skies]]' vassals. As some of the best archers in the world, for generations, Hachut tribespeople served as mercenaries in the wars of the continent.

Hachua is mostly rocky upland and forests bounded by the Krina River and Atah Highlands. It is located to the east of [[Prakan]], around the Gulf of Flowers, and is neighboured by [[Etetipawan|the Etetipawan]] in the north, and [[Chupash]] to the east.

##### Three Skies

Though long splintered and worshipping various faiths, in the Hela clan have recently finished uniting the clans under their banner, and the [[True Faith]], with the support of Three Skies.
