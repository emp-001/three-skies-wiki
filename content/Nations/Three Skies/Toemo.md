##### ***Delta Farmers***

***Ken Tamwa*, or the Tributary of Toemo**, is one of [[Three Skies]]' vassals. It is located to the immediate west of [[Sila]], on the fringes of [[Etetipawan|the Etetipawan]], and the Golden Plains.

##### Three Skies

The Kingdom of Toemo broke away from the Kingdom of Sila, under a branch of their royal family. 

They immediately offered tribute to Three Skies, in exchange for protection. Three Skies obliged, and when Sila sent a force to bring Toemo back into the fold, Tolkan riparines were sent in force to defend Toemo and subjugate Sila.
