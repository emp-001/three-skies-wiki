##### ***Gateway to the North***

*This article is a stub. It may be expanded on in future.*

***Ken Roenwa*, or the Tributary of Aroen,** is one of [[Three Skies]]' vassals. It is centred around a large city in the mountains on the easternmost edge of Three Skies influence, controlling the main passage north to the [[Uwced|Empire of Uwced]]. The city is among the largest on the planet, and a dominant centre for commerce and the arts, called by some the Root of the World.

It sits next to a large freshwater lake in the highlands of the Goejon Mountains along the Blood Coast, at the very terminus of the Great Canal network. The people there have shared heritage with [[Sila]], and speak a dialect of their language Silan.

##### Three Skies

Aroen submitted to Three Skies a few decades ago, after its ruler, lacking heirs of her own, bequeathed the city to the league in her will. The Tolkans then appointed a friendly minor house as Archons.
