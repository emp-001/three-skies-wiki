##### ***Wounded Giant***

***Wamat Uwced*, or Empire of Uwced,** is an nation to the north and east of [[Three Skies]], and one of the largest nations on the planet. Once a beacon of prosperity and order, it is a shadow of its former self, with its blood guilds and regional magnates wielding greater power than the ruling Uwceg Brood itself.

Still, it controls vast natural resources and goods that cannot be found anywhere else on the continent, and can call upon a vast population to fight if threatened. It is the main rival to Three Skies' hegemony on the continent.

## Origins

The origin myths of Uwced are as numerous as the hundred peoples who call the land home. Tales of floods, of great infernos, of the gods planting the people there like seeds, are all common.

The empire itself though formed over centuries, the gradual sediment of tribe after kingdom after empire, each one growing larger and more powerful, until Uwced came to dominate the region and its many lakes some thousand years ago. During this golden age, it ruled over one hundred million souls and an army the likes of which the world has never seen, before or since. Its hegemony lasted for centuries, and word of its power could be heard as far west as [[Prakan]].

Centuries ago, the Cataclysm of Amsyr rent the land. One of the mountains to the north erupted, causing great destruction and bringing about a winter that would last a decade. Scores of millions perished, and the empire was never the same.

So great was this cataclysm, that the land itself shattered, and the great lakes Hawn, Lyryon, Ekau, Kwaed, Eriad and Kuch that once formed the imperial heartland were connected together by new rivers and waterways, becoming known as the Imperial Sea. They were tainted, as the water that flowed down from the Rina Mountains was now poisoned, some say by the sins of the Uwceg that came before.

The Sea can give no life: its salts are poisonous, only jellies and sickened fish can be found in its waters, and the farmland fed by rivers flowing south produces a poor crop. Only the one lake disconnected from this network, Emon, remains untainted, by the grace of the Brood.

## Geography

Uwced occupies the far northeast of the continent, in a vast peninsula between the Blood Coast and Roiling Sea. In the north of this region, a vast network of lakes and rivers form the Imperial Sea. These formed after a cataclysm hundreds of years ago, which connected many scattered lakes together, and in the process they were tainted and polluted, inhospitable to life.

Many lands in and around the sea have been abandoned, allowing nature to reclaim them. Further north, a vast rainforest dominates the remaining uncontaminated land, and is called the Shield Forest. The tribes there pledge fealty to the Brood, but little else.

The largest lake outside of the Imperial Sea is Emon, which is untouched by the taint that has affected the rest of the land, and is where the largest Uwceg cities can now be found; the imperial core.

Owing to its fertility before the cataclysm, Uwced is where some of the earliest cities on the continent formed. The ruins of the largest cities the world has seen can be found there, like the old capital, Ekau-Kurwen.

Uwced is bounded by the Rina Mountains to the west and north, the Gryma River to the west, the Blood Coast to the south, and Roiling Sea to the east.

## Leadership

The empire does not have a sole emperor. It is ruled by the Brood, the descendants of its founders, who have maintained a bloodline stretching back centuries and claim to be the children of God herself.

The Brood is less a family and more a polycule. They act as one, fulfilling roles in government like war, coin and faith, like they were chores in a household. Collectively and individually, they are addressed as *Umang*, or 'Sovereign'.

The Brood goes through cycles of adopting new blood, and remaining closed, which can last for decades at a time.

There are no formal limits on the Brood's divinely mandated power, but in practice, it relies on various factions and power brokers to maintain control, each with their own interests:

##### Ay, the Blood-Guilds

Every citizen of the empire is a part of an *Ay*, or blood guild. They are part university, part company, part guild, tied to a particular element like water, gold and iron.

Some blood-guilds have a particular focus: a profession or role, such as smithing or farming, but most cover many purposes within the territory granted to them. They have a strict hierarchy, with their leaders typically drawn from *Proy* nobles.

Because guilds manage mating and reproduction collectively as well, the guilds are seen as ones family, moreso than any blood relation. Guild members will have symbols of their guild tattooed onto their skin.

The Ay are the largest owners of slaves on the entire continent, as they own all but a few of the farms and mines in the empire. Though many slaves can claim descent from captives taken in ancient wars, most nowadays are indentured - debtors who have sold themselves into bondage.

Each Ay is formally sponsored by a member of the Brood.

##### Proy

The Proy are the descendants of the regional and local governors called Uthy, appointed by the Brood to manage their empire. As the grasp of the Brood weakened, these gradually became hereditary positions, sometimes mimicking the Brood itself, cast small.

They serve the Brood still, out of faith, pride or patronage, but they are forever jostling for power amongst themselves, and have a great deal of autonomy deciding how to interpret the Brood's will.

##### Idaef, the Ay of Paper

The *Idaef* are the imperial bureaucracy. Once, they had agents across the whole empire, but now they are restricted to just the imperial core around Lake Emon.

A blood-guild of their own, they select meritocratically, and are one of the sole means of social mobility in the empire. Though loyal to the Brood, they have years of experience manipulating them, pushing the empire in the direction they want.

##### Ehaen, the Ay of Iron

The *Ehaen* are the vestiges of the old imperial cohorts, known for their ceremonial gorgets and tall iron helms. Once a professional standing army, the Ehaen of today is effectively a mercenary force led by the Brood, that they rent out to members of the Proy to resolve disputes and fight petty wars. Still, the force brutally roots out threats to the Brood, and the sight of their distinctive helms instil fear to this day. They are a blood-guild, and their element is iron.

## Religion  
  
Religion in Uwced is subsumed entirely by the Brood. They are seen as demigods, the immortal bloodline of the original One God, who saved the world from destruction by her sisters, the *Oedon*, aeons ago.

They have countless traditions and rituals, but most are based on the idea that the physical blood of the Brood maintains a barrier preventing the return of the Oedon.

A formal Temple exists to worship the Brood and the One God, managed by the Ay of Purity.

Beyond this, countless smaller faiths and folk religions exist across the empire. These are tolerated, so long as due reverence is given to the Brood and their cult.

## Culture  
 

##### Values

At all tiers of society, the Uwceg value submission and respect. The realm is seen as an extended family, with chains of elders, parents and children who must show deference to those above them.

As a result, physical punishment is favoured over execution for crimes, based around an idea that rulers must show a sort of paternalism to their subjects.

The upper classes have a language of their own, and strict etiquette around conversation and behaviour.

Despite this traditionalism, there is an abiding sense that the Uwceg have been punished for abandoning their values, causing the empire to fall into decline - and moral panics are frequent and violent.

##### Gender

Gender norms in Uwced differ greatly from those in Three Skies. Gender, and the ability to reproduce, are seen as things to be earned, rather than assigned. Blood-guilds reserve the sole right to grant gender, doing so based on their own obscure laws and regulations.

As such, there is less of a gender hierarchy, and more a divide between those who may contribute to offspring, and those who may not.

Typically, there are a limited number of Koras an Kimas within a guild-rank, and these are seen as jobs to be filled. Service to the Ay can earn someone the right to ascend, and contribute to a shared creche of offspring.

This system has to be maintained by the guilds, so in more rural regions, local traditions seep in.

##### Dress & Appearance

Styles of dress vary greatly across the vast empire of Uwced, and customs may be different even across blood-guilds.

That being said, Uwceg around Lake Emon typically have scales of mercury or silver, with grey and mousy hair and eyes of pale green and blue. They are not shorter or smaller than other ethnicities, but their nobility prizes a slender figure.

Fashion in the upper classes is defined by coverage and layering. There are several traditional named layers of clothing, each attached to a different caste of society. Nobles will wear upwards of five layers, though ten layers and more are the reserve of the Brood.

The natural wealth of the land is present in the vast array of materials incorporated into outfits. Currently, sleek, monochrome fabrics with subtle patternwork are in vogue, as are tall brimless hats made of felt. Jewellery of carved precious stones is preferred over gold and silver.

The lower classes - free commoners and tradespeople - will wear between two and four layers, usually over a romper or jumpsuit of sorts, with a coloured sash or harness. In cooler climes, this is paired with a fur vest and hat.

At formal occasions, even those of modest standing wear a wide belt bearing a circular relief - a carved stone or jewel - depicting their achievements within their guild. This is added to over their life.

##### Customs

###### Blood-Guild Initiations  
Entry into blood-guilds as an outsider, or coming of age as a child of a member is an important ceremony. These are held three times a year, when the moon is in a certain phase. The actual ritual depends on the guild, but a visible mark or tattoo is made on the hands or face to represent ones membership, and there is a great feast afterward, following a period of fasting for the inductee to represent the prosperity they will have inside the guild.

## Economy

Despite the cataclysm that wracked it years ago, Uwced remains a powerhouse, with resources that can be found nowhere else on the planet, and high quality goods prized across the continent.

Because of this, they need only import food and salt, having outgrown their arable land, and they do so by selling luxury finished goods and gems, of which they have a surplus.

They are highly protective of their iron, as they have the only source on the continent; a huge strategic advantage. All iron is stamped with the seal of the Sovereign.

## Relations  
  
Dominating its corner of the map, Uwced has only vassals and distant rivals, but no neighbours.

The western and eastern Keymun kingdoms south of the Gryma River are nominally tributes of the Brood, but their loyalty is questionable.

The Three Skies vassal [[Aroen]] is its closest neighbour, past the Gryma River. Tensions have risen since Aroen fell into the Tolkan sphere.
