##### ***Woodland Clans***

*This article is a stub. It may be expanded on in future.*

***Kemmiphit Ahwat*, or the Unbroken Line of Ahwat,** or simply the Confederation of Ahwat, is a confederation of tribes within the vast Hewati forests in the centre of the continent, and a neighbour of [[Three Skies]]. Fiercely protective of their lands and gods, the Ahwat fought for decades with the league before unifying and securing their independence.

Today, the Ahwat maintain peace with Three Skies by allowing logging in their outer forests, for two months a year. They maintain this is not tribute.

Ahwat is located east of [[Tuklua]] and north of [[Etetipawan|the Etetipawan]] and contains the source of the vital Krina River. The Batanki river runs along its eastern border with [[Latti]]. All of Ahwat is densely forested, with the land split between the colder Upper Weald and the temperate Lower Weald.

The land lacks any real permanent settlements, as its people are nomadic hunter-gatherers and pastoralists that live in mobile structures which hang in the tree canopy, or in caves.

For the most part, individual clans rule independently, but the Council-of-Twenty-and-Three may call the whole forest to arms if threatened.

The Ahwat have a strong martial tradition, and train in the use of the short spear and javelin. Those from the Upper Weald are known to raise sabre-tooth Ontok-cats for combat.

Despite continued incursions by [[True Faith|missionaries]] from Three Skies, Ahwat remains a pagan stronghold, where dozens of small faiths and cults practice.
