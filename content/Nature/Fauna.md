The planet of this setting is different to ours. There are no real beasts of burden and no horses, and the sun and the composition of the mantle have given many things a metallic shimmer.

However, in practice, players will need real-world comparisons to make sense of the world, so it is alien, but not in a frustrating way.

Therefore, when an animal species is referenced, it will often have some real-world equivalent next to it, to contextualise it and give players a quick idea of what form the creature takes. For example, a *Tippu-Bird*, or an *Ēku-Dog*. Once players are familiar with the name, this suffix can be dropped.
