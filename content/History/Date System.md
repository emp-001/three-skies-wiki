## A Note on Date Systems

The peoples of the continent have countless ways of marking time and recording history. Even within Three Skies, where such things are beginning to be standardised, there is a diversity of calendar systems.

This setting is not Earth. The length of days, seasons and years will not be the same as ours. That being said, bespoke timekeeping systems often cause more trouble than they are worth in real gameplay.

That is why any games in this setting will translate the diegetic calendar systems in-universe to the days, weeks, months and years that we would understand.

## Epoch Markers

One exception to this rule are historical eras. To give a sense of consistent history, the setting breaks dates into eras.

In Three Skies, the most commonly used era system is tied to the True Faith, with years marked as 'Of the Reclamation' (OR) after the birth of the faith in Oluk, and 'In Shadow' (IS) before. This reflects the Faithful belief that they are reclaiming the world and rebuilding the strength of the Total-God.

These are appended before each year. For example: if someone was born 52 years before the liberation of Oluk, they were born in IS 52. If that same person died 12 years after the Faith was born in Oluk, they died in OR 12.

In-universe, these epoch markers aren't text, but symbols next to numbers:

IS is represented as an empty circle, and OR as a six-pointed star.
