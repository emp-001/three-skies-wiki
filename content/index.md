##### *The year is 400 of the Reclamation. The League of Three Skies dominates the continent. The New Tongue can be heard from Iruchu to distant Aroue. Daily do the Faithful bring pagans onto the righteous path. And in the cities of Prakan, new wonders of science and engineering carve out a new future for the world.*

##### *But all is not well. The current Chancellor, de facto ruler of Three Skies, is dying. She was one of the Golden Circle, a group of six champions who led the League out of a civil war and into its current golden age, half a century ago. Now only she remains. When she passes, several factions are poised to seize power, coiled like rabid dogs just waiting for their moment, and all of them long for war.*

##### *Unbeknownst to all, a cabal of immortals, fugitives from a distant planet, fight a shadow war. Long have they known that their kin know where they are and seek bloody revenge.*
